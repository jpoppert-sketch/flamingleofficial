The Movement

One Symbol, One Mission, and Official Places to Flock.

The Symbol

The Flamingo.

The world loves putting people in boxes — orientation, type, preference, a dozen categories before you've even said hello. The flamingo skips all of it. One symbol, for every single person. Wearing it means one thing: off the apps, open to something real. Put it on, join the movement.

Wear the Flamingo. Spot the Flamingo. Say Hi.

The Places

Flock Spots.

An Official Flock Spot isn't just a venue that opted in — it's a partner that believes in the movement and supports you. A place where meeting someone in real life can be a bit more comfortable and fun. Not a mixer with nametags. Not a first date with a stranger you met on the apps. Just a go-to spot for Flaminglers to become regulars.

Why Now

Dating Culture Is Broken.

It doesn't take much more than a scroll through social media to land on the same conclusion: dating apps have killed dating. People are giving up — sick of the revolving door of one-off matches, conversations that go nowhere, and getting ghosted by someone they never even met in person.

The numbers back it up. A recent Forbes Health survey found 78% of dating app users report burnout, and 40% say they can't find a genuine connection on the apps at all. Denver — Flamingle's home base — has one of the highest concentrations of single adults of any major U.S. city, roughly 6 in 10 adults unmarried, per Census data. The timing isn't a guess. It's already happening.

The Mission

We Aim to Fix It.

At its core, the mission is simple: create a culture and community where people are comfortable approaching each other in real life again — weaning people off dating apps and changing the narrative of dating culture in a positive direction. Not a swipe. Not a match. Just two people, in the same place, willing to say hi.

Join the Movement.

Let's fix dating culture, one flamingo at a time.
