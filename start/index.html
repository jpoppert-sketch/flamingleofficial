The whole idea

Three moves. That's the movement.

01

Put it on when you go out

Heading to a bar, a coffee shop, a show, a park? Slide on the coral. It says one thing: off the apps, open to something real.

02

Spot the coral on someone else

Same band, same meaning. You just found someone who's also here to meet people in person — and who's already told you it's okay to come say hi.

03

Walk over. Say hi.

Either person can start it. “Nice flamingo” works. The band did the hard part — you just have to show up.

You've got two to give away.

Hand them to single friends who are tired of swiping. The flock grows one wrist at a time — and it's more fun when the people you know are wearing it too.
