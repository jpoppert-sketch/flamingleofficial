The Pitch

What Is Flamingle™?

Flamingle™ ("You Single? Let's Flamingle.") is a brand, a movement, and a growing ecosystem designed to help singles get off the apps and back into the real world. The core mechanic is simple: branded merchandise that signals I'm single and open. One symbol, one meaning. Strangers see the flamingo and know they have permission to approach.

The app is the digital layer that connects the physical movement — scannable profiles, an opt-in heat map of nearby Flaminglers, event discovery, and a community feed. Think less dating app, more movement infrastructure.

The brand is built, the merch is launching, and the pilot is Denver. This role is Denver-local.

What You're Building

A Fully Scoped Product Waiting to Be Built.

The product requirements document is complete. The brand, visual identity, and color system are established. You're not walking into a napkin idea — you're walking into a real spec with a real brand behind it.

  • Flamingle Card — scannable digital profile shared person-to-person via QR. No stranger browsing. Connections only with people you've actually met.
  • "I'm Out" Mode — anonymized opt-in heat map showing nearby Flaminglers and Flamingle Friendly venues.
  • Double-blind contact exchange — neither party's contact info is revealed until both confirm. Mutual opt-in only.
  • Event discovery — local Flamingle events surfaced in-app.
  • "Spotted in the Wild" feed — UGC photo submissions from the community.
  • Flamingle Friendly venue profiles — commercial partners register in the app, manage their profile, post weekly events, and list Flamingle discounts. Venues appear on the "I'm Out" heat map when a flamboyance is forming nearby.
  • Merch store integration — Shopify embedded in-app.
