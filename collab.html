The Pitch

What Is Flamingle™?

Flamingle™ ("You Single? Let's Flamingle.") is a brand, a movement, and a growing ecosystem designed to help singles get off the apps and back into the real world. The core mechanic is simple: branded merchandise that signals I'm off the apps, open to something real. One symbol, one meaning. Strangers spot the flamingo and know they have permission to approach.

The merch is live. The app is live. The venue partner network is signing its first bars. This isn't a pitch deck — it's a real, running thing that needs someone to tell its story. Think less dating app, more movement.

We're based in Denver and running our pilot here. This role is Denver-local.

Who We're Looking For

You Live and Breathe Social Content.

Someone who already understands the Denver social scene, gets the humor and heart behind this brand, and wants to build something from the ground up.

  • A skilled content creator and storyteller — particularly on TikTok and Instagram
  • Deep understanding of Denver's social scene and vibe

The Role

Chief Content Officer

You lead the content presence of Flamingle across three platforms — each with its own format and purpose. TikTok and Instagram are the short-form engine: fast, funny, cultural, shareable. YouTube is the long-form home: real conversations about modern dating, connection, the science of attraction, and why swiping broke something that didn't need to be broken.

You're not executing a content calendar someone else built. You're building the strategy and owning the creative direction, with the full support of the founder on brand, product, and business infrastructure. This is a real collaboration — you bring the content expertise and street-level presence, we build everything around it together.

Content pillars (starting point — you'll shape these):

Real-world connection stories & interviews
How to Flamingle (field guide in video form)
The science of attraction & modern dating
Flamingo Friendly venue features
Flock spotting in the wild
Brand humor & self-aware storytelling

Platform breakdown: TikTok + Instagram — short-form, trend-driven, cultural awareness. YouTube — long-form interviews, expert conversations, positive connection stories, the "why" behind the movement.

Time commitment: 15–20 hours per week to start, with a clear path to full-time salaried as the brand grows.

The Founder

Joel Poppert is a lifetime entrepreneur — from running a construction company in his twenties, to leading clean energy initiatives, to co-founding a private finance company. He builds things. That's what he does.

Off the clock, he's out living the kind of life Flamingle is built for — traveling, hiking, catching live music, finding a good patio, and never turning down a dog. Especially Mesa, his own.

He met his ex-wife on OkCupid. They're amicably divorced. For the four years since, he's had what he describes as a love-hate relationship with the apps — mostly hate. He's old enough to remember what it felt like to walk up to someone in the wild, heart pounding, and say hi. The awkwardness of it. The rejection. And those rare moments of genuine connection and butterflies that no algorithm has ever been able to replicate.

Those years also sent him on a personal journey. He's put real work into developing emotional intelligence — a deep value for authenticity, human connection, and self-awareness. That's not a footnote. It's the foundation this brand is built on.

Flamingle isn't a product. It's a belief that we've broken something that didn't need to be broken — and that we can get back to the fundamentals of how people actually connect. He's building the infrastructure. He needs someone to build the culture around it.
