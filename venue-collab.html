Flamingle™ is a real-world singles movement launching in Denver. Become a Flamingle Friendly venue and put a built-in audience of social, open singles through your door — for free.

The Movement

Singles Are Getting Off the Apps.

Flamingle™ is a growing community of adults who are done swiping and ready to meet people in the real world. The mechanic is simple: wearing a flamingo means you're single and open. One symbol. Everyone knows what it means.

These are social, outgoing people actively looking for a reason to get out of the house. They're your customer. We're just making it easier for them to find you.

What You Get

Free. Simple. No Strings.

  • Flamingle Friendly window cling — goes in your window. Flaminglers walking by know you're a safe, welcoming spot.
  • Your own venue profile in the Flamingle app — name, location, weekly events, and any Flamingle discounts you want to offer. Flaminglers nearby will find you.
  • Featured on the "I'm Out" heat map — when a flamboyance starts forming in your neighborhood, your venue shows up as the place to be.
  • A built-in community event audience — host a happy hour, trivia night, or any weekly event and it surfaces directly to Flaminglers in your area.
  • Zero cost to join — this is a partnership, not an ad buy.
Optional But Powerful

The Flamingle Discount.

Offer a discount to anyone wearing Flamingle merch — a pin, a hat, a tee — and you've just turned your venue into part of the signal. They wear it, they get something. You get a customer who's already in a social, open mindset and happy to be there.

It doesn't have to be much. A dollar off a drink. A free appetizer with two. Whatever makes sense for your business. The point is the moment — someone walks in wearing a flamingo, your staff recognizes it, and suddenly your bar is part of the story.

How It Works

Three Steps to Flamingle Friendly.

1

Who This Is For

Bars, Coffee Shops, Breweries, Event Spaces.

Any venue where adults gather socially is a fit. The best Flamingle Friendly spots are places with a regular weekly event — a trivia night, a happy hour, a live music night — that gives the community a reason to show up on a schedule.

You don't need to run a Flamingle-branded event. You just need to be a place where people feel comfortable walking up to a stranger and saying hi. If that's your vibe, you're already most of the way there.
